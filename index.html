Common questions

Everything you need
to know.

Do you travel outside of Nashville? +
Absolutely — I'm available worldwide. Travel fees apply for events more than 60 miles from Nashville, and I'm happy to discuss destination weddings and elopements anywhere in the world.
How many photos will we receive? +
Most full-day weddings result in 500–800 fully edited images. Elopements typically deliver 200–400, and engagement sessions deliver 75–150. Quality always comes before quantity.
When do we get our photos back? +
Your fully edited gallery is delivered within 6–8 weeks via a private online gallery you can download, share, and order prints from at any time.
What if something happens on our wedding day? +
I always carry backup equipment and have a trusted network of photographers I can call on in a genuine emergency. Your day is always covered — I take that responsibility seriously.
Do you shoot both film and digital? +
I primarily shoot digital to ensure reliability and a full gallery delivery, but I'm happy to discuss film add-ons for couples who love that look. Just mention it in your inquiry.
